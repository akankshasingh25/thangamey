Step three

While you're here

Both afternoons are free between roughly two and five. Puri rewards an idle afternoon more than most places.

  • The beach is right there

    Swimming is best early, before the sun turns unreasonable. The sunrise over the water is the thing people come for, and it is genuinely worth setting an alarm.

  • The Jagannath temple

    Ten minutes from either hotel and the reason Puri exists. Non-Hindu visitors can't go inside, but the roof of the library across the road is the view everyone photographs anyway.

  • Raghurajpur

    A village of pattachitra painters about half an hour out, where nearly every house is a studio. If you liked the paintings on your invitation, go here.

  • Eat the obvious things

    Fish, prawns, and whatever the hotel is calling the local thali. Ask anyone about the sweets — you'll get a forty-minute answer and a strong recommendation.
