Step 3

Roam Around

We don't know how you will find time but if you do, you can do these in and around the city. We can share number of our transport vendor in case you want to travel outstation.

  • The Jagannath Temple

    Ten minutes from either hotel and the reason Puri exists. We are arranging for "fast-service" for our guests, let us know when you intend to go to the temple.

  • Bhubaneswar

    If you are passing through, the Lingaraj Temple, the Mukteshwar Temple and the Rajarani Temple are all there.

  • Satapada, for Chilika Lake

    About 40–50 km from Puri. It is migratory bird season, so keep a hat, sunglasses and phones charged.

  • Raghurajpur

    About 11 km from Puri, a village of pattachitra painters. Come back with a painting.

  • Konark Sun Temple

    About 35 km from Puri. Go for the temple and stay for the light and sound show in the evening.
